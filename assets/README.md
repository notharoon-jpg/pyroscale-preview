## Earth imagery

- `earth-blue-marble.jpg` is the NASA Blue Marble texture distributed with [three-globe](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three-globe/example/img/earth-blue-marble.jpg). Credit NASA Goddard Space Flight Center and the [NASA Earth Observatory Blue Marble team](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/).
- `earth-night.jpg` is the Earth at night texture distributed with [three-globe](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three-globe/example/img/earth-night.jpg).
- `earth-blue-marble-hd.webp` is an 8192 × 4096 WebP resized from [NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, August 2004, with topography and bathymetry](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/). Credit Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth Observatory.
- `milky-way.webp` is a resized and display-tinted version of [The Milky Way panorama](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/). Credit ESO/S. Brunier. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- `country-boundaries.json` contains simplified coordinates from the [Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 land boundaries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-boundary-lines/), distributed in the public domain.
- `nasa-modis-hotspots.jpg` and `nasa-viirs-hotspots.jpg` show active fire detections in northeast China on November 6, 2015, from [A Clearer View of Fire](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/a-clearer-view-of-fire-87111/). NASA images by Jeff Schmaltz and Jesse Allen, LANCE/EOSDIS Rapid Response.
- `earth-landing-fallback.jpg` is the [Earth by Suomi NPP](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/earth-by-suomi-npp/) image (VIIRS). Credit NASA/NOAA/GSFC/Suomi NPP/VIIRS/Norman Kuring.

The globe uses [Three.js r128](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r128). Its browser build, OrbitControls, and MIT license are in `vendor/three-r128/`.
