        Method and data notes

        This globe counts NASA FIRMS thermal-anomaly detections in monthly 3° cells. An anomaly can be a fire pixel, a gas flare or another heat source. Repeated overpasses can count the same fire more than once.

        Terra MODIS, Suomi-NPP VIIRS and NOAA-20 VIIRS are separate observed series. The experimental estimate places earlier Terra counts on a Suomi-NPP-like scale where the fit has support. Archive-day checks identify gaps in the record. Cloud-free coverage varies.

        Reading the map

        Each glow marks the centre of a 3° cell, roughly 330 km north to south. It represents aggregated detections across the cell.

        The forecast estimates NOAA-20 detections. The activity layer uses the same calendar month in the NOAA-20 history for comparison.

        Countries and cities

        Pick any countries and cities, as many as you like. Country filters use Natural Earth boundaries. A cell spanning a border contributes by area to country summaries. City selections group nearby cells.
